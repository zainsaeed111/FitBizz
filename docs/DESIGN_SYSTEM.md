# Design System — FitBizz (Japani Phal & White Commercial Identity)

The canonical source of truth for FitBizz visual design across Mobile, Desktop, and Web.

## 1. Aesthetic Direction & Brand Identity
* **Brand Name**: FitBizz
* **Color Inspiration**: Japani Phal (Persimmon / Kaki) — Rich Deep Persimmon Coral Red, Warm Orange-Terracotta, Sunburst Amber, contrasted with Pure Snow White and Warm Ivory.
* **Visual Persona**: *Warm, Energetic, Premium, High Contrast, Rich Japani Phal Gradients, Commercial Fitness Authority*.

---

## 2. Color Palette Tokens

### Japani Phal (Persimmon) Brand Palette
* **Primary Japani Phal Coral**: `#EA580C` (Persimmon Red-Orange)
* **Bright Persimmon Gradient**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #f97316 0%, #ea580c 100%)`
* **Deep Terracotta Persimmon**: `#C2410C`
* **Warm Amber Persimmon**: `#F59E0B`
* **Soft Persimmon Cream Tint**: `#FFEDD5` (Orange 100)
* **Background Canvas**: `#FAFAF9` (Stone 50) / `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
* **Card Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Snow White)
* **Default Border**: `#E7E5E4` (Stone 200) / `#FED7AA` (Orange 200)
* **Primary Heading Text**: `#1C1917` (Warm Obsidian Stone 900)
* **Body Text**: `#44403C` (Stone 700)
* **Muted Text**: `#78716C` (Stone 500)

### Status & Feedback Tokens
* **Success**: `#15803D` (Mint Green) / `#DCFCE7` (Background)
* **Warning**: `#B45309` (Amber) / `#FEF3C7` (Background)
* **Error / Danger**: `#B91C1C` (Crimson) / `#FEE2E2` (Background)
* **Info / Tier**: `#EA580C` (Japani Phal) / `#FFEDD5` (Background)

---

## 3. Surface & Interaction Rules

1. **Japani Phal Primary Buttons**: Linear gradient from Vibrant Persimmon Orange (`#F97316`) to Deep Persimmon Coral (`#EA580C`) with warm drop shadow (`rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.25)`).
2. **Active Navigation Pills**: Japani Phal gradient fill with white bold text.
3. **Surfaces**: Crisp white panels with subtle warm stone borders (`#E7E5E4`).
